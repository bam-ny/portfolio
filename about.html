About Me

Originally from Thailand and now based in Vancouver, I'm a UI/UX designer with a background in graphic design and a passion for creating thoughtful, intuitive digital experiences.

I combine research, problem-solving, and visual design to transform complex challenges into simple, meaningful solutions. My work explores how design and emerging technologies can improve everyday experiences and make digital products more accessible and engaging.

As a designer, my goal is to continue growing, exploring new technologies, and collaborating with people from diverse backgrounds. I aspire to create impactful digital products that not only solve real-world problems but also make a positive difference in people's lives.
