About Me

Originally from Thailand and now based in Vancouver, I'm a Product Designer with a background in graphic design and a passion for creating thoughtful, intuitive digital experiences. My journey from graphic design to product design has shaped how I approach challenges, allowing me to balance creativity, functionality, and user needs.

I combine research, problem-solving, and visual design to transform complex challenges into simple, meaningful solutions. I believe good design goes beyond aesthetics. It's about understanding people, identifying their needs, and creating experiences that make their everyday lives easier. I'm particularly interested in exploring how design and emerging technologies can make digital products more accessible, engaging, and impactful.

As a designer, my goal is to continuously grow, challenge myself, and explore new ideas and technologies. I enjoy collaborating with people from different backgrounds and disciplines, learning from their perspectives, and discovering new approaches to problem-solving. Ultimately, I aspire to create meaningful digital products that solve real-world problems, connect people, and make a positive difference in their lives.
